Once upon a time, a little fox lived beside a river. Every morning the fox walked to the water and watched the fish. The sun was warm and the river was clear.
One day a small bird landed on a stone. The bird asked the fox to help find a seed. They looked under the leaves and beside the tall tree. At last the fox found a seed near the water.
The bird planted the seed in the soft ground. The fox brought water from the river. They waited through the rain and the sunshine. A green leaf appeared, and then a bright flower opened.
Every morning the fox and the bird sat beside the flower. They told stories about the trees and the stars. When the sun went down, the fox returned home and the bird flew to its nest.
A little rabbit lived on the other side of the river. The rabbit wanted to visit the flower but could not swim. The fox found a fallen branch and made a small bridge. The rabbit crossed the bridge slowly.
The three friends shared a meal beside the tall tree. The bird brought seeds, the rabbit brought carrots, and the fox brought water. They were happy to be together.
When winter came, the river grew cold. The flower dropped its seeds on the ground. The friends gathered some seeds and kept them safe in a little wooden box. They knew that spring would come again.
In spring the rabbit opened the box. The bird carried the seeds to the garden. The fox watered the ground. Soon the garden was full of flowers, and the three friends sat in the sunshine.
Once upon a time, a child found a small stone beside a lake. The stone was smooth and blue. The child put it in a pocket and walked home through the woods.
At home the child showed the stone to a friend. They decided to paint a picture of the lake. They painted blue water, green trees, and a bright yellow sun. The picture reminded them of a happy day.
